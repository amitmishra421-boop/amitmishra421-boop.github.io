The Unseen Why Studio Tools

The Unseen Why Studio Tools are private automation tools used only by the owner of the YouTube channel The Unseen Why (@theunseenwhy-u3m). They are not a public service, and no other person can sign in or use them.

What the tools do

  • Publisher: uploads the owner's approved videos, sets captions, thumbnails and metadata, schedules publishing, and manages playlists through the official YouTube Data API.
  • Analytics Collector: reads the channel's own statistics and YouTube Analytics reports (read-only) so the owner can review performance.
  • Community Engagement: reads comments on the channel's videos and posts replies only after the owner has individually approved each reply.

All tools run on Google Apps Script under the owner's Google account and store their records in the owner's own Google Sheets and Google Drive.

Read the Privacy Policy to learn how Google and YouTube account data is handled.
