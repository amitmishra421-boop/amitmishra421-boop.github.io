Privacy Policy: The Unseen Why Studio Tools

Last updated: 25 November 2026 · https://amitmishra421-boop.github.io/privacy.html

1. Who uses these tools

The Unseen Why Studio Tools (the Publisher, the Analytics Collector and the Community Engagement tool) are private applications used only by the authorised owner of The Unseen Why YouTube channel. They are not offered to the public and no other users can authorise them.

2. Google user data accessed

  • Publisher: the authorised YouTube channel's account and channel information, videos, uploads, metadata, captions, thumbnails, playlists and scheduled publishing settings.
  • Analytics Collector: read-only channel and video statistics and YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's channel.
  • Community Engagement: public comments and replies on the channel's own videos, and the ability to post replies that the owner has approved.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to perform the functions the owner has authorised: publishing and scheduling the owner's videos, keeping approval records, preventing duplicate uploads, monitoring publishing status, reviewing channel performance, drafting and posting approved replies, and diagnosing operational errors.

4. Storage and security

The tools run on Google Apps Script under the owner's Google account. Operational records are stored in the owner's own Google Sheets and Google Drive. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in Apps Script properties that only the owner's account can access; they are never written to sheets, logs or any third-party service.

5. Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with any third party. Data is sent only to Google and YouTube services, and only as needed to perform the authorised functions.

6. Retention and deletion

Operational records are kept for auditing and troubleshooting until the owner deletes them. Stored tokens are deleted when a tool is disconnected. The owner can delete all stored records at any time from their own Google Drive, and can revoke each tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube features are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Contact

Privacy enquiries can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google OAuth consent screen.
